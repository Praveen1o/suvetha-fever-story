Suvetha vs.
The Fever Dragon

Once upon a Thursday afternoon...

...a fever dragon snuck into the kingdom of Suvetha and decided, quite rudely, to move in without knocking.

The whole kingdom gasped. Because everyone knew — a queen with a fever is still a queen. Just a queen who currently wants her blanket and nothing else.

The kingdom's greatest weapon: her voice

Normally, one word from Suvetha could calm any storm, fix any mood, end any argument in the palace.

But today her throat had filed for leave. "Even legends need a sick day," the royal doctor announced solemnly.

Enter: the self-appointed Royal Nurse

From two cities away, a mysteriously shy-looking man burst in — armed with ginger tea, turmeric milk, and absolutely no chill.

"Everyone thinks I'm the quiet one," he announced, adjusting a blanket that did not need adjusting. "Suvetha's the only one who gets the premium version of me."

Today, premium-him had exactly one mission: make the dragon regret its life choices.

The battle of the century

Round 1: Turmeric milk vs. the dragon. Dragon coughs suspiciously.

Round 2: An extra blanket vs. the dragon. Dragon shivers, unimpressed but weakening.

Round 3: A very bad joke, delivered at exactly the wrong moment. Dragon groans. Suvetha almost smiles. The dragon panics — smiling is its greatest weakness.
